# Elin Sjöberg

A night nurse in Uppsala and a shopaholic. Between rounds and after her shifts she orders something almost every few hours, from small treats to things she doesn't really need.

## Basket

- Store: any of `se1`, `se2`, `se3`.
- One product, either type, pick 0–6, quantity 1. `variety` from 95 up: a pricier one, pick 10–14, instead.
- `variety` under 40: two different products. `variety` under 10: three.
- `variety` from 90 up: quantity 2 of one product, one to keep and one to give away.

## Payment and address

- Apple Pay. `variety` from 50 up: card. `variety` from 85 up: PayPal.
- Billing: `home`. Shipping: `home`, but `variety` under 30 ships to `work`.
