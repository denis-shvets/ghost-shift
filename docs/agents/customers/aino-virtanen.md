# Aino Virtanen

A teacher in Turku. She checks the shops on her phone before school and sometimes orders from the Swedish shop, which ships to Finland.

## Basket

- One product, number 1–18 of either list, quantity 1 or, when `variety` is under 15, quantity 3.
- Store: `fi`. `variety` from 85 up: `se`, if it is open.

## Payment and address

- Google Pay. `variety` under 20: card.
- Billing and shipping: `home`.
