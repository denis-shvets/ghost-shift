# Jonas Weber

A dentist in Hamburg who buys rarely but well, usually on a weekend, and often as a present.

## Basket

- Store: any of `de1`, `de2`, `de3`.
- Two to four products, either type, picks 8–14 (the dearer half), quantity 1 each. `variety` under 50: two products, otherwise three; `variety` under 5: four.

## Payment and address

- Amex. `variety` from 60 up: bank transfer.
- Billing: `home`. `variety` under 40: ship to `gift`; otherwise to `home`.
