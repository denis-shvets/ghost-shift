# Jonas Weber

A dentist in Hamburg who buys rarely but well, usually on a weekend, and often as a present.

## Basket

- Two to four products from the top of the lists, numbers 18–25 of either list, quantity 1 each. `variety` under 50: two products, otherwise three; `variety` under 5: four.

## Payment and address

- Amex. `variety` from 60 up: bank transfer.
- Billing: `home`. `variety` under 40: ship to `gift`; otherwise to `home`.
