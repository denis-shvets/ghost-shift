# Felix Brandt

A freelance stylist in Cologne and a shopaholic. He works from home, browses the shop between clients all day, and orders pieces several times a day, for himself and for his clients.

## Basket

- Store: `de1` or `de2`, whichever he picks first for the visit.
- One physical product, pick 4–12, quantity 1.
- `variety` under 50: add a second product, either type. `variety` under 15: add a third.
- `variety` from 80 up: quantity 2 to 4 of one physical product, pick 0–6 (`variety` 80–89: 2, 90–96: 3, 97–99: 4), for a client.

## Payment and address

- Card. `variety` from 60 up: PayPal. `variety` from 90 up: bank transfer, for a client.
- Billing and shipping: `home`. Client orders (`variety` from 80 up): `workshop` for both.
