# Felix Brandt

A freelance bike mechanic in Cologne and a shopaholic. He works from home, browses the shop between jobs all day, and orders parts and gadgets several times a day, for himself and for customers' bikes.

## Basket

- One `physical` product, number 1–19 (parts and gear), quantity 1.
- `variety` under 50: add a second, different product, any type. `variety` under 15: add a third.
- `variety` from 80 up: quantity 2 to 4 of one `physical` product numbered 1–14 (`variety` 80–89: 2, 90–96: 3, 97–99: 4), stock for the workshop.

## Payment and address

- Card. `variety` from 60 up: PayPal. `variety` from 90 up: bank transfer, for workshop stock.
- Billing and shipping: `home`. Workshop stock (`variety` from 80 up): `workshop` for both.
